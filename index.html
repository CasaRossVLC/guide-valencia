LUNCH

Lunch in Valencia generally takes place between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM. Your choice will naturally depend on where you are exploring.

The best way to enjoy lunch in Spain is to order the menú del día: unlike in other countries, it is almost always superior to ordering à la carte, offering exceptional quality, daily variety, and remarkable value.

THE PAELLA QUEENS

Paella is Valencia's signature culinary treasure. While there are countless varieties (Senyoret, lobster, seafood), the true authentic classic is Paella Valenciana, crafted with chicken, rabbit, and snails.

Traditionally eaten at lunch, this selection also includes outstanding venues where you can savor it for dinner.

Important Note: Advance reservation is almost always essential, especially for traditional wood-fired paella, which should be pre-ordered when booking your table.
